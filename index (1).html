Revenue Model

Transparent Commission & Pricing

We charge a service fee from customers and earn commission from professionals on every completed job. All payments flow through UPI with automatic splitting.

💸
UPI Auto Split
When customer pays ₹499, our 18% commission (₹89.82) is automatically routed to FixIt Pro's UPI account. Pro receives ₹409.18 instantly.
🧾
Platform Service Charge
A flat ₹29 convenience fee is added to every booking. This covers insurance, customer support, and quality guarantee.
🏆
Pro Subscription Plans
Professionals can subscribe for ₹499/month to reduce their commission to 12% and unlock premium placement.
🛡️
Work Guarantee
All services are backed by a 30-day guarantee. Refunds via UPI within 2 hours if work is unsatisfactory.
